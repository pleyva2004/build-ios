# Designing this app by talking to Claude

The app runs in the Simulator next to Claude. Describe what you want and the screen updates in a few
seconds. No code, no waiting for builds.

## Talking to Claude
- **Say exactly what you want when you know it:** "make the score label bigger", "rename the button
  to Improve my score", "put the percentage next to the name".
- **Describe the feeling when you're exploring:** "make it calmer", "more premium", "less anxious
  about the score". Claude shows **3 different options**, so you choose instead of describing.
- **Point instead of describing:** type **select**, tap the thing in the Simulator, then say what to
  change about it ("make this warmer").
- **One idea per message** is fastest. Several small messages beat one long one.

## Words that work instantly (no waiting)
| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| undo | takes the last change back |
| redo | puts it back again |
| next / previous | flips between the options Claude showed |
| option 2 | jumps to that option |
| keep | keeps the option on screen |
| select | then tap an element to talk about it |

## Your taste is remembered
Say it once: "always use serif type", "never use red", "from now on keep the cards dark". Claude adds
it to the app's design brief and follows it in every later change, in this and future sessions.

## What needs a developer
Animations, maps, charts, swipe gestures and custom drawing. Claude will say when a request needs
one of these. Everything else (colors, type, spacing, layout, copy, new screens, buttons and
navigation) you can do yourself.
